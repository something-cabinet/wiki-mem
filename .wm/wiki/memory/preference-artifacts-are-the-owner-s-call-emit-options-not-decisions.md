---
title: Preference artifacts are the owner's call - emit options, not decisions
type: memory
tags: [process, authority]
status: active
---

When a deliverable encodes the owner's taste or authority (which rules bind everyone, which variant is canonical, what is shared vs local), produce options with a labelled recommendation and BLANK decision fields. Test: if the artifact stays correct after the owner flips every recommendation, it is an options document; if flipping one breaks it, you made a decision for them.
